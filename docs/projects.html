Light Canvas

Completed project · Published paper

Turning a flat surface into an interactive drawing space through computer vision and IoT.

Project notes +

The problem

Applied ML2023 · continuing evaluation

AutoML for air quality & disease burden

Research prototype

Examining environmental and health data, with attention to generalization across countries.

Project notes +

The problem

Predictive performance on randomly split data may conceal failures when a model is applied in a different setting.

My contribution

I developed a machine-learning framework and examined country-grouped validation to expose generalization weaknesses, motivating revised evaluation and SHAP-based interpretation.

Methods & tools

Python, AutoML, SHAP, grouped validation, distribution-shift analysis

Outcome

An analysis and evaluation framework documented in my latest CV. This prototype is separate from peer-reviewed publications.

Robotics & embedded systems2022

Autonomous delivery bot

Completed prototype

A small delivery prototype that follows a predefined line, combining embedded control and cargo transport.

Project notes +

The problem

A delivery task can be a practical way to connect sensing, control, mechanics, and reliable movement along a known route.

My contribution

I developed a prototype with a cargo chamber and line-following sensors, using Arduino and embedded control to follow a predefined path.

Methods & tools

Arduino, line-following sensors, embedded control, IoT

Outcome

A working delivery-bot prototype documented in my earlier CV. This is line-following autonomy; broader navigation capabilities are not claimed.

Adaptive mobile-banking interfaces

Completed research · Published paper

Using interaction behavior to adapt a mobile-banking interface.

Project notes +

The problem

A fixed mobile interface may not suit the needs and interaction patterns of every user.

My contribution

I developed and evaluated a machine-learning-driven adaptive interface using user behavior, combining predictive modeling, clustering, SHAP-based explanation, and human-centered evaluation.

Methods & tools

Machine learning, clustering, SHAP, adaptive interfaces, statistical evaluation

Outcome

Published in Array, volume 30, article 100901 (2026).

Human–AI interaction2023–2024 · HCI-E 2025

Web3 vs Web2 user experience & PLS-SEM tool

Completed study · Accepted paper

A prototype-based comparison of user experience in decentralized and conventional social media.

Project notes +

The problem

Decentralized social platforms introduce interaction choices that need to be evaluated from users’ perspectives.

My contribution

I designed a prototype to evaluate UX differences between Web3 and Web2 social platforms. This work also includes a PLS-SEM tool, as part of the Web3/Web2 paper.

Methods & tools

Comparative HCI evaluation, prototyping, System Usability Scale, PLS-SEM tool

Outcome

Accepted and presented at HCI-E 2025; the paper is in press for Springer CCIS. Detailed documentation of the analysis tool and a public software link will be added when available.

LLMs & agentic AIOngoing research

Multimodal multi-agent AI

Work in progress

Specialized agents that share multimodal evidence and verify intermediate reasoning.

Project notes +

The problem

An unreliable intermediate answer can propagate through a multi-agent workflow and shape later decisions.

My contribution

I am developing a LangGraph-based framework in which specialized agents process evidence, exchange context, coordinate reasoning, and perform cross-agent verification.

Methods & tools

Python, LangGraph, multimodal evidence, agent coordination, failure analysis

Outcome

Ongoing research on information sharing, verification, and error propagation. No benchmark improvements or completed evaluation results are claimed.

Human–AI interactionOngoing research

LLM & VLM trust comparison

Work in progress

Investigating how uncertainty and high-confidence errors shape trust in language and multimodal models.

Project notes +

The problem

A confident answer can appear convincing even when it is wrong. Appropriate reliance requires more than persuasive language.

My contribution

I am investigating how model confidence, uncertainty communication, explanations, and high-confidence errors influence human reliance on LLMs and VLMs.

Methods & tools

Human-centered evaluation, comparative model analysis, uncertainty and trust calibration

Outcome

Ongoing investigation. Comparative findings and study materials will be shared once available.

Computer vision & explainable AIOngoing research

Trustworthy skin-lesion classification

Work in progress

Studying how medical-image models can communicate uncertainty and support careful human review.

Project notes +

The problem

A classifier’s prediction alone does not explain when its output may be unreliable or how a clinician should interpret it.

My contribution

I am developing a DINO-ViT-based framework on HAM10000, incorporating uncertainty quantification, visual explanations, fairness analysis, and clinician-centered evaluation.

Methods & tools

DINO-ViT, HAM10000, uncertainty quantification, visual explanations, fairness analysis

Outcome

Ongoing research. Final performance, clinical validation, and deployment outcomes have not been established.
