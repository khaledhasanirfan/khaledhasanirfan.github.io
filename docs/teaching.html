Teaching & mentorship

Undergraduate teaching and research mentorship at the Department of CSE, MIST.

My approach

Connect the idea with the implementation.

I want students to connect the ideas behind a system with the choices involved in building it. In programming and systems courses, I emphasize clear reasoning, implementation, and the habit of testing assumptions. Laboratory work gives students space to experiment, debug, and explain their decisions. In research and thesis supervision, I encourage careful problem formulation, appropriate evaluation, and honest interpretation of results. My aim is to help students become independent learners who can communicate technical work clearly, ask useful questions, and recognize the limits of what their evidence supports.

Research & thesis mentorship

I co-supervise undergraduate research, CSE-400 theses, and CSE-350 industrial training. I support students through problem formulation, implementation, experimentation, analysis, and technical communication.

My teaching and mentoring has involved more than 220 students across theory and laboratory sections, as documented in my latest CV.
