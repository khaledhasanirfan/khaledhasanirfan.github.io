Khaled Hasan

Lecturer, Computer Science & Engineering
Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST)

I study how language models and intelligent agents can reason, collaborate, and remain reliable in the real world.

I am a lecturer and researcher at MIST, Bangladesh. My primary research focus is LLM/VLM systems and agentic AI, with an emphasis on multimodal reasoning, uncertainty, evaluation, and failure analysis.

My work also connects human–AI interaction, explainable machine learning, and reliable edge and sensing systems. I enjoy building systems, examining where they fail, and learning across these connected areas.
